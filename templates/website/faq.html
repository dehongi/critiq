Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about using Critiq.

Critiq is a platform where movie enthusiasts can share their opinions on films through reviews and ratings. Users can browse movies, read reviews from other users, and contribute their own thoughts on the films they've watched.

You can browse movies and read reviews without an account. However, to write reviews, rate movies, or add new movies to the database, you'll need to create a free account.

To create an account, click on the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner of the page. You'll need to provide a valid email address and create a password. After registering, you'll receive a verification email to confirm your account.

If you've forgotten your password, click on the "Log In" button and then select "Forgot Password?" on the login page. Enter your email address, and we'll send you instructions to reset your password.

To add a new movie, log in to your account, navigate to the Movies page, and click the "Add Movie" button. Fill out the required information about the movie, including title, release year, director, and synopsis. You can also upload a poster image for the movie.

To write a review, first find the movie you want to review by browsing or searching. On the movie's detail page, scroll down to the review section and click "Write a Review." You'll need to provide a title for your review, a rating (1-10), and your written review content.

Yes, you can edit or delete your own reviews at any time. To do so, navigate to the review and look for the edit or delete buttons. You can also manage all your reviews from your "My Reviews" page accessible from the Reviews dropdown in the navigation menu.

We support JPEG, PNG, and GIF file formats for movie posters. The recommended image size is 500x750 pixels. The maximum file size allowed is 5MB.

Yes, we take data security seriously. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your personal information. We never share your data with third parties without your consent. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.
