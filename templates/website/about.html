About Critiq

Our Story

Critiq was founded in 2023 by a group of passionate movie enthusiasts who wanted to create a platform where people could share their honest opinions about films and discover new movies based on authentic reviews.

What started as a small project has grown into a vibrant community of movie lovers from around the world, all united by their passion for cinema and storytelling.

Our Mission

At Critiq, our mission is to create a space where movie enthusiasts can:

  • Share their honest opinions about films they've watched
  • Discover new movies based on authentic reviews from real people
  • Connect with others who share their taste in cinema
  • Engage in meaningful discussions about the art of filmmaking

We believe that everyone's opinion matters and that the best movie recommendations come from people who share your taste in films.
