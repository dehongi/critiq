Account Help

How to Create an Account

  1. Click on the "Sign Up" button in the top-right corner of any page.
  2. Enter your email address and create a password.
  3. Check your email for a verification link and click it to activate your account.
  4. Complete your profile information (optional).

Managing Your Profile

To update your profile information:

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Click on your username in the top-right corner.
  3. Select "Profile" from the dropdown menu.
  4. Click "Edit Profile" to make changes.

Password Recovery

If you've forgotten your password:

  1. Click "Log In" in the top-right corner.
  2. Select "Forgot Password?" below the login form.
  3. Enter your email address and submit the form.
  4. Check your email for password reset instructions.

Account Security

We recommend the following security practices:

  • Use a strong, unique password for your Critiq account.
  • Never share your login credentials with others.
  • Log out when using shared or public computers.
  • Update your password regularly.

Movies & Reviews

Adding a New Movie

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to the Movies page.
  3. Click the "Add Movie" button.
  4. Fill out the movie details form with title, release year, director, and synopsis.
  5. Upload a poster image (optional).
  6. Click "Add Movie" to submit.

Writing a Review

  1. Find the movie you want to review.
  2. On the movie's detail page, scroll to the review section.
  3. Click "Write a Review" (or use the review form if available).
  4. Enter a title for your review.
  5. Select a rating from 1 to 10.
  6. Write your review in the content field.
  7. Click "Submit Review" to publish.

Managing Your Reviews

To edit or delete your reviews:

  1. Go to "My Reviews" from the Reviews dropdown in the navigation menu.
  2. Find the review you want to manage.
  3. Click "Edit" to modify the review or "Delete" to remove it.
  4. If editing, make your changes and click "Update Review" to save.
  5. If deleting, confirm your decision on the confirmation page.

Review Guidelines

When writing reviews, please follow these guidelines:

  • Be respectful and constructive in your criticism.
  • Avoid spoilers or use spoiler warnings.
  • Don't include offensive language or personal attacks.
  • Focus on your experience with the film and explain your rating.
  • Reviews that violate our community guidelines may be removed.

Technical Support

Supported Browsers

Critiq works best with the following browsers:

  • Google Chrome (latest version)
  • Mozilla Firefox (latest version)
  • Safari (latest version)
  • Microsoft Edge (latest version)

For the best experience, please ensure your browser is updated to the latest version.

Image Upload Issues

If you're having trouble uploading movie posters:

  • Ensure the file is in JPEG, PNG, or GIF format.
  • Check that the file size is under 5MB.
  • Try resizing the image to 500x750 pixels.
  • Clear your browser cache and try again.

Page Loading Problems

If pages are loading slowly or not at all:

  1. Check your internet connection.
  2. Clear your browser cache and cookies.
  3. Disable browser extensions that might interfere.
  4. Try using a different browser.
  5. If the problem persists, contact our support team.

Reporting Bugs

If you encounter a bug or technical issue:

  1. Note the specific actions that led to the problem.
  2. Take a screenshot if possible.
  3. Note your browser and device information.
  4. Contact us through the Contact Form with these details.
